Why Kaylee's projection moved · 21:55 → 25:25

Two reasons. The prediction engine was rebuilt to grade climbing, rolling and descending separately and apply this course's terrain to each — retiring the old 21:55, which leaned on a single fast, smooth, low-vertical race. And Kaylee's complete race history was re-scored to terrain — not her one best day — alongside her two most recent ES100-type workouts, where that smooth-course speed doesn't carry.

A note on confidence

Every projection is only as good as its data — quantity, recency and accuracy. These carry no physiological data from either athlete — above all none from Justyna, the focus here — and only limited detailed workouts and splits from Justyna. Read them as the honest shape of the day; they tighten as her physiology, splits and the Ironstone read come in.
