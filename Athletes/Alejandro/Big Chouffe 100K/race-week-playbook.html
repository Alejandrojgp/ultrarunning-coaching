Part 2 — Race-week nutrition & carb loading

Built on current sports-nutrition consensus: a 36–48 h glycogen load at 8–12 g carbs/kg/day, spread across 5–6 smaller low-fibre feeds rather than a few large meals. At 71 kg that's your daily load-day target. Over ~12 g/kg only adds GI distress without extra usable glycogen. Expect +1–2 kg on the scale — that's glycogen-bound water (every gram of glycogen holds ~3 g water) and it burns off during the race; do not panic about it.

Load-day food toolkit (low-fibre, gut-friendly)

  • White rice, white pasta with simple/low-fat sauce
  • White bread, bagels, plain rolls, pretzels
  • Boiled/mashed white potato (no skin)
  • Bananas (ripe), honey, jam, maple syrup
  • Oats (fine, not steel-cut), rice cakes
  • Pancakes/waffles, low-fibre cereal
  • Sports drink, fruit juice, Maurten / drink mix
  • Lean protein in modest portions: eggs, chicken, white fish, Greek yogurt

Ease off on load days (Jul 2–3)

  • High-fibre: whole grains, raw veg/salad, beans, nuts, skins
  • High-fat/greasy or very spicy foods
  • Anything new or untested — nothing novel race week
  • Excess alcohol (impairs glycogen storage + sleep)

Hydration & sodium pre-load

  • All week: pale-yellow urine as the gauge. Carry a bottle on travel days — cabin air dehydrates.
  • Fri Jul 3: sip electrolyte fluid through the day (the extra carbs + glycogen need water to store). Add a concentrated electrolyte (LMNT / The Feed) to ~1 of your bottles.
  • Race morning: ~500 ml with electrolytes 2–3 h pre-start; small sips after. Don't over-drink plain water (dilutes sodium).
  • Caffeine: hold intake moderate Jul 2–3 so race-day caffeine (~240 mg plan) lands fresh.

Travel-day eating

  • Tue Jun 30 (overnight flight): eat a real carb-forward dinner before/at the airport. On board: hydrate continuously, keep eating light carbs, skip alcohol, melatonin for sleep. Don't let the travel day become an accidental fast.
  • Wed Jul 1 (arrival): get a normal carb-rich meal on Belgian meal-times immediately — eating on local schedule helps reset the body clock as much as light does.

Gembloux prep → carry (no kitchen at Houffalize; no eating out the last day)

You have prep ability in Gembloux (Jul 1–2) but the Houffalize hotel has no kitchen and you're avoiding restaurants on Jul 3. So pre-make and carry your Jul 3 dinner and Jul 4 breakfast.

  • Shop in Gembloux Jul 1–2: white rice/pasta, bread/bagels, bananas, honey/jam, instant oats, rice cakes, juice/sports drink, eggs or pre-cooked chicken
  • Cook Jul 2 in Gembloux: a big batch of plain white rice or pasta + a simple sauce + lean protein — portion into containers
  • Carry to Houffalize Jul 3: the cooked rice/pasta (your low-residue dinner), breakfast makings (oats, banana, honey, coffee), and snacks; a small cooler bag if available
  • Confirm the room has: kettle/microwave or hot-water access for oats and to warm the rice/pasta — if not, plan a no-heat dinner (bagels + honey + banana + yogurt)

Race-morning breakfast — Sat Jul 4 (~03:00, ~3 h pre-start)

Tested & familiar (~150–200 g carb): oats (made with water/some milk) + ripe banana + generous honey + a bagel/toast with jam + coffee. Sip ½ Maurten 320 flask in the last 30 min. This mirrors your long-run rehearsal breakfasts — nothing new.

06:00 start will feel like the middle of the night on Florida time — the early breakfast + caffeine plan is built for it. If appetite is low at 03:00, lean more liquid: sports drink, juice, a gel, and the Maurten flask.
