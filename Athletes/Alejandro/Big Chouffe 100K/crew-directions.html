Read first — how crewing works here. The 100K is run in semi-autonomy. There is ONE deposited drop-bag point: Engreux-Camping, km 50 (drop the bag at race HQ / Vayamundo before the start — the organizers move it there). In-person crewing is allowed: the aid posts are reachable for supporters, so you can meet Alejandro and hand him supplies at the road-accessible stations below. Confirm the exact supporter-accessible list + parking + any shuttle/road-closures at bib pickup (Fri Jul 3) — ask for the "supporters / roadbook" sheet. Then drive every leg with Waze or Google Maps live; the roads below are the spine, not turn-by-turn.
