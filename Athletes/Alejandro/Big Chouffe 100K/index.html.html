Target: 90 g carbs/hr · 500–750 ml fluid/hr · 700–850 mg sodium/hr

Gut-failure protocol

If GI distress kicks in (cramping, nausea, urge to vomit):
  1. Stop solid food immediately
  2. Switch to sports drink only, 200 ml every 15 min
  3. Walk for 5 min to reset peristalsis
  4. If symptoms persist 30 min, add ginger chew + cool water on stomach
  5. Reintroduce gels slowly after symptoms clear; skip real food until next aid

DNF early-warning signals

If TWO or more of these show up before AS5, dial back pace 1 min/km for one segment, reassess at next aid:
  • HR climbing on flats at otherwise easy effort
  • Can't take in carbs (gels feel disgusting)
  • Quads hot 60K out from finish
  • Mental focus deteriorating — making poor route-finding decisions
  • Stopped sweating in conditions where you should be

This is not failure. A pace re-set at AS3 saves the race. Ignoring these signals is what causes DNFs.
